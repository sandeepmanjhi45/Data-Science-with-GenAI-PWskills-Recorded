# What is Cloud? (Simple Notes)

## What is the cloud?
The cloud means using someone else's powerful computers over the internet, so you don't have to buy and run your own.

**Example:** When you save a photo to Google Drive, it is stored on Google's computers, not only on your phone. That is the cloud.

## Three simple ideas
- **Use it when you need it:** you don't have to buy any machines first.
- **Make it bigger or smaller:** if more people visit your website, you can add more power. When they leave, you can reduce it.
- **Pay only for what you use:** just like an electricity bill.

## Why do companies use the cloud?
1. **It saves money.** No need to buy expensive servers, and the cloud company looks after repairs and updates.
2. **It grows easily.** If a website suddenly gets a lot of visitors, it keeps working.
3. **It is fast.** Teams can launch their apps quickly using ready-made tools.

## Three types of cloud service

| Type | Simple meaning | Examples |
|------|----------------|----------|
| **SaaS** | You just use the ready-made app | Gmail, Google Docs, Microsoft 365 |
| **PaaS** | You get a place to build and run your own app | Heroku, Google App Engine |
| **IaaS** | You rent the raw computer, storage and network | Amazon EC2, Azure Virtual Machines |

**Easy way to remember (pizza example):**
- SaaS = you order a ready pizza and just eat it.
- PaaS = you get the kitchen and ingredients and make the pizza yourself.
- IaaS = you only rent an empty space and bring everything else.

## Big cloud companies
- **AWS (Amazon):** the biggest one, with 200+ services.
- **Microsoft Azure:** popular with big companies and Windows users.
- **Google Cloud:** also very popular.

## Remember
- Cloud = renting computing power over the internet.
- SaaS is the easiest to use, and IaaS gives you the most control.
